# E11-5: AICL web pages, instance management, and cross-surface proof

## Acceptance criteria
E11-5.AC1 maps F3-S4,F3-S5,F3-S3,F3-S7,F3-S9,F2-S1,F2-S2,F2-S3,F2-S6; test and browser.

## Dependencies
E11-3, E11-4.

## Design
Owns E11 wiring and the management UIs: read projections, Connections area, instance detail with every mutation form, simulated-work form and confirmation prompt, and the production composition (`create_app`, `python -m aicl.test_server`, `npm run start:test`). Features: aicl-experience (F3-S3, F3-S4, F3-S5, F3-S7, F3-S9) and the connection scenarios whose UI is delivered here (F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6).

## Solution approach
Build `WorkspaceView`, `InstanceView` and `ConnectionsView` from the read operations of the other contracts and the Connections, detail, activation, limits, capability, attachment, domain, resolve-gate, simulated-work and confirmation forms as listed in the experience document's ownership table. Assemble `create_app` and the test server and write the minimal browser journeys on the production-wired composition (fakes only at provider, executor, chat model, email and clock). Every journey carries AC annotations.

## Contracts
Consume every E11 contract; own the read projections and the production wiring in `docs/design/aicl-experience/technical.md`. No new contract is introduced here; wiring criterion E11-5.AC1 in design.json.

## Verification
Browser journeys on the production-wired composition (no in-test replacement of internal services): sign-in, wizard and chat creation with equivalent results, activation, limits and capability and attachment through the fake OAuth page, simulated work settled and shown in usage, over-limit gating shown with its reason, confirmation-required send denied then confirmed once, executed once and denied on replay, revocation detaching and gating, owner-domain add and removal, another tenant's isolation, and redacted audit history. Integration tests assert the projections and that the wired app registers every route. Backend gate rules are not re-tested here.
