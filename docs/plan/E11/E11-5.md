# E11-5: AICL web pages, instance management, and cross-surface proof

## Acceptance criteria
E11-5.AC1 maps F3-S4,F3-S5,F3-S3,F3-S7,F3-S9,F2-S1,F2-S2,F2-S3,F2-S6; test and browser.

## Dependencies
E11-3, E11-4.

## Design
Owns the management UIs and the proof that the production composition is wired: read projections (`WorkspaceView`, `InstanceDetailView` composing the E11-2 `InstanceSnapshot`, `ConnectionsView`), Connections area, instance detail with every mutation form, simulated-work form and confirmation prompt, and the wiring proof plus production-wired journeys. The `create_app` skeleton, `python -m aicl.test_server`, `npm run start:test`, `backend/requirements.txt`, `package.json` and its lockfile are owned by E11-1; E11-5 only adds its own router and ports to them. Features: aicl-experience (F3-S3, F3-S4, F3-S5, F3-S7, F3-S9) and the connection scenarios whose UI is delivered here (F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6).

## Solution approach
Build `WorkspaceView`, `InstanceDetailView` (snapshot plus budget, usage, audit and pending actions) and `ConnectionsView` from the read operations of the other contracts and the Connections, detail, activation, limits, capability, attachment, domain, resolve-gate (button **Re-check and clear**: clears the gate only when the control now permits work, otherwise shows the `gate_unresolved` denial and the gate stays), simulated-work and confirmation forms as listed in the experience document's ownership table. Register the E11-5 router, then prove that `create_app(settings)` with `ports=None` registers every route and every real port of E11-1 to E11-5 and fails startup when one is missing. Write the minimal browser journeys on the production-wired composition (fakes only at provider, executor, chat model, email and clock; real sign-in, not the seeded session). Every journey carries AC annotations.

### Internal slice order (no ticket split)
1. Projections and forms: the three views, every mutation form, simulated-work form and confirmation prompt. Tests (E11-5.AC1; F3-S4, F3-S5, F3-S7, F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6): integration tests for the projections (tenant filter, USD rendering, audit allow-list only, `InstanceDetailView` composing the snapshot) and that each form posts `expected_version` and CSRF to the E11-2 operation and shows the committed result or the structured denial, including the `gate_unresolved` denial.
2. Journeys and wiring proof: the production-wired composition test (every route and port registered, missing port fails startup) and the browser journeys below (F3-S3, F3-S9). Tests: one journey series with real sign-in covering the listed flows.
A ticket split is a Planning delta and is used only if Development finds this ticket undeliverable in a bounded loop; none is made now.

## Contracts
Consume every E11 contract; own the read projections and the production-wiring proof in `docs/design/aicl-experience/technical.md`. No new contract is introduced here; wiring criterion E11-5.AC1 in design.json.

## Verification
Browser journeys on the production-wired composition (no in-test replacement of internal services; real magic-link sign-in): sign-in, wizard and chat creation with equivalent results, activation, limits and capability and attachment through the fake OAuth page, simulated work settled and shown in usage, over-limit gating shown with its reason, **Re-check and clear** leaving the gate in place while the limit is still zero and clearing it after the limit is raised, confirmation-required send denied then confirmed once, executed once and denied on replay, revocation detaching and gating, owner-domain add and removal, another tenant's isolation, and redacted audit history. Integration tests assert the projections and that the production app registers every route and port. Backend gate rules are not re-tested here.
