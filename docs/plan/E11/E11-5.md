# E11-5: AICL web pages, instance management, and cross-surface proof

## Acceptance criteria
E11-5.AC1 maps F3-S4,F3-S5,F3-S3,F3-S7,F3-S9,F2-S1,F2-S2,F2-S3,F2-S6; test and browser.

## Dependencies
E11-3, E11-4.

## Design
Owns the management UIs and the proof that the production composition is wired: read projections (`WorkspaceView`, `InstanceDetailView` composing the E11-2 `InstanceSnapshot`, `ConnectionsView`), Connections area, instance detail with every mutation form, simulated-work form and confirmation prompt, and the wiring proof plus production-wired journeys. The `create_app` skeleton, `python -m aicl.test_server`, `npm run start:test`, `backend/requirements.txt`, `package.json` and its lockfile are owned by E11-1; E11-5 only adds its own router and ports to them. Features: aicl-experience (F3-S3, F3-S4, F3-S5, F3-S7, F3-S9) and the connection scenarios whose UI is delivered here (F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6).

## Solution approach
Build `WorkspaceView`, `InstanceDetailView` (snapshot plus budget, usage, audit and pending actions) and `ConnectionsView` from the read operations of the other contracts and the Connections, detail, activation, limits, capability, attachment, domain, resolve-gate (button **Re-check and clear**: clears the gate only when the control now permits work, otherwise shows the `gate_unresolved` denial and the gate stays), simulated-work and confirmation forms as listed in the experience document's ownership table. Register the E11-5 router, then prove that, with the test flags on (test composition), `create_app(settings)` with `ports=None` resolves every required port of the control-plane `Ports` list, registers every `ROUTE_FACTORIES` entry and every `STARTUP_HOOKS` hook (sweeper, reconciler, magic-link purge) and fails startup when a port is missing; this is test-composition evidence, since production differs only by the absent test-only routes, capture sender, seeded session and fake OAuth page. Write the minimal browser journeys on the production-wired composition (fakes only at provider, executor, chat model, email and clock; real sign-in, not the seeded session). Every journey carries AC annotations.

### Internal slice order (no ticket split)
1. Projections and forms: the three views, every mutation form, simulated-work form and confirmation prompt. Tests (E11-5.AC1; F3-S4, F3-S5, F3-S7, F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6): integration tests for the projections (tenant filter, USD rendering, audit allow-list only, `InstanceDetailView` composing the snapshot) and that each form posts `expected_version` and CSRF to the E11-2 operation and shows the committed result or the structured denial, including the `gate_unresolved` denial.
2. Journeys and wiring proof: the production-wired composition test (every route, port and startup hook registered with the test flags on, missing port fails startup) and the browser journeys below (F3-S3, F3-S9). Tests: one journey series with real sign-in covering the listed flows.
A ticket split is a Planning delta and is used only if Development finds this ticket undeliverable in a bounded loop; none is made now.

## Contracts
Consume every E11 contract; own the read projections and the production-wiring proof in `docs/design/aicl-experience/technical.md`. No new contract is introduced here; wiring criterion E11-5.AC1 in design.json.

## Verification
Browser journeys on the production-wired composition (no in-test replacement of internal services; real magic-link sign-in): sign-in, wizard and chat creation with equivalent results, activation, limits and capability and attachment through the fake OAuth page, simulated work settled and shown in usage, over-limit gating shown with its reason, **Re-check and clear** leaving the gate in place while the limit is still zero and clearing it after all four limits are raised to positive values (a zero limit is exhausted even for a zero-cost category), confirmation-required send denied then confirmed once, executed once and denied on replay (the replay resubmits the same payload with a new `work_id` and gets `confirmation_used`), revocation detaching and gating, owner-domain add and removal, another tenant's isolation (the non-empty list and the chat versus wizard equivalence are E11-5's), and redacted audit history. Integration tests assert the projections and that the test composition registers every route, port and startup hook. Backend gate rules are not re-tested here.
