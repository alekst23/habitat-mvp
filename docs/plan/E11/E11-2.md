## Acceptance criteria

## Dependencies
E11-1.

## Design
Owns the canonical creation service and first-party web-app tools (`create_instance`, `InstanceSnapshot`, `InstanceOperations` including `evaluate_gate_control` and `resolve_gate`, tool registry), plus connection and network policy: connections, OAuth, secret store, credential broker, `NetworkPolicy` (pure `check_destination_rules`, `classify_addresses` and resolver-based `authorize_destination`), the `WorkAuthorization` dispatch gate, the work executor port with its deterministic fake, the hold reconciler and the simulated-work and confirmation routes. It registers its routers and real ports through the E11-1 extension point and tests them through the E11-1 `create_app` and test server. Features: aicl-control-plane (F1-S14 to F1-S16) and connection-network-policy (F2-S1 to F2-S11).

## Solution approach
Add `backend/aicl/creation/`, `connections/`, `network/` and `work/`. `create_instance` validates against the one-template catalog and writes the draft instance, grants and audit event in one `db.transaction()` with `(tenant_id, idempotency_key)` idempotency, and returns the lean `InstanceSnapshot`. `InstanceOperations` implements `set_lifecycle`, `set_capability`, `set_limits`, add and remove network domain, `resolve_gate`, `attach_connection` and `detach_connection` with `expected_version`; every mutation that can fix a control, and `resolve_gate` for every reason (delivered per slice below), call `evaluate_gate_control` and clear the gate only when the control now permits work (otherwise `denied` with `gate_unresolved`; a window rollover never clears). `network_denied` is persisted only for the owner-resolvable allow-list miss (`host_not_allowed` for `network.fetch`); a structurally invalid destination denies that request, is audited with its rule and persists no gate (a design interpretation of D11-8, see the epic). The tool registry exposes only `list_templates`, `create_instance` and `read_instance` (returning `InstanceSnapshot` only) at `POST /app-tools/{name}`. Implement the OAuth begin and callback with state, PKCE and scope checks against `FakeProviderOAuth` (its authorize page registered only in test and development compositions), the AES-256-GCM `SecretStore`, the credential broker and the revocation phases. `WorkAuthorization` runs the fixed ten-step order; step 9 composes `BudgetPort.reserve(conn, ...)`, `consume_confirmation(conn, ...)` and the insert of the `WorkRequestRecord` (replay returns the stored `WorkResult`, a different digest is `conflict`) in one transaction, and a denial writes `gated_reason` and its audit event in their own transactions. The `RevocationSweeper` and the `HoldReconciler` are registered in the E11-1 `STARTUP_HOOKS` (sweeper first, each with `run_once()`; a sweeper failure fails startup, a reconciler exception is audited and holds keep blocking; single process). The reconciler (`reconcile_holds`, `AICL_RECONCILE_INTERVAL_SECONDS`) moves a stale `held` hold through `mark_unknown` then `reconcile`, or `release`s it when `WorkExecutorPort.lookup` returns `not_started` (also the result for a `work_id` never seen). `execute` records `started` for the `work_id` before any side effect (also in `FakeWorkExecutor`), so releasing on `not_started` is safe and started-unrecorded work is `still_unknown`; a `settle` on a `released` hold is `conflict` with a `budget.late_settlement` audit event.

### Internal slice order (no ticket split)
This ticket is broad. Development delivers it in these slices, each merged to the ticket branch with its own tests, all tied to the existing E11-2.AC1 scenarios (one integration test may serve several). Later slices may rely on earlier ones only.
1. Creation, operations and tools (F1-S14, the lifecycle, capability and limit edits of F1-S15, `/app-tools/*`): `create_instance`, `InstanceSnapshot` with an empty attachment list, the tool registry, `set_lifecycle`, `set_capability`, `set_limits`, `evaluate_gate_control` and `resolve_gate` for the capability and budget reasons only. Tests: idempotency, conflict, validation and the key pattern; tool schemas rejecting tenant, owner, credential and confirmation arguments; version conflicts; `resolve_gate` for `capability_disabled`, `budget_exhausted` and `budget_unavailable` (denied `gate_unresolved` while the control denies, cleared when it permits, not by a rollover).
2. Connections, OAuth, secret store and broker (F2-S1, F2-S2, F2-S3, F2-S6, F2-S7, F2-S10): connections, `ConnectionAttachment`, `attach_connection`, `detach_connection`, snapshot attachments, the `connection_*` re-check in `evaluate_gate_control` and `resolve_gate`, OAuth, secret store, broker and revocation. Tests: attach rules, OAuth state, PKCE, replay, expiry and scope shortfall, revocation including provider failure and the crash sweeper (`run_once()`), `resolve_gate` for the `connection_*` reasons, no credential in any result, audit event or log.
3. Network and SSRF (F2-S4, F2-S5, F2-S8, and the domain half of F1-S15): `normalize_owner_domain`, `add_network_domain`, `remove_network_domain`, the `network_denied` re-check (`host_not_allowed` only), `check_destination_rules`, `classify_addresses` and `authorize_destination`. Tests: unit tests of those functions with the fake resolver, domain add and remove with version conflicts, `resolve_gate` for `network_denied`.
4. Gate, executor, reconciler and routes (F1-S16, F2-S9, F2-S11, and the end-to-end proof of F1-S5, F1-S6, F1-S9): gate order and each denial, the atomic step 9 (a failed reservation does not burn the confirmation and the reverse, a lost consume race is `confirmation_used`), `WorkRequestRecord` replay, digest `conflict` and outcome updates with each settle, unknown, release and reconcile, confirmation single use, expiry, payload mismatch and the confirmed resubmission with the payload carried by the form, executor `started` recording and `still_unknown`, over-measured and unknown outcomes, late settle of a released hold (`conflict` plus audit), stale `held` and unseen `work_id` reconciliation through `run_once()` with `FakeClock`, pause or archive denying new work, startup rejection of non-fake modes, HTTP tests of the callback, work-request and confirm routes.
A ticket split is a Planning delta and is used only if Development finds this ticket undeliverable in a bounded loop; none is made now.

## Verification
Integration tests through the real service and `/app-tools/*` (E11-1 `create_app` and test server) per slice above: creation idempotency, conflict and validation; tool schemas rejecting tenant, owner, credential and confirmation arguments; lifecycle, capability, limit, domain and version-conflict operations; `resolve_gate` re-evaluation for each gate reason; attach rules; OAuth state, PKCE, replay, expiry and scope shortfall; revocation including provider failure and the crash sweeper; gate order and each denial with atomic step 9; confirmation single use, expiry and payload mismatch; over-measured and unknown outcomes through the fake executor; reconciler handling of stale `held` and unseen `work_id`; no credential in any result, audit event or log; startup rejection of non-fake modes. Unit tests cover host normalization, `check_destination_rules` and address classification. E11-2.AC1 also maps F1-S5, F1-S6 and F1-S9 and proves their end-to-end behavior (denial reported as `gated` through the dispatch gate, pause or archive denying new work with history visible, the persisted gate cleared only by the fixing mutation or `resolve_gate`); E11-1 proves only the primitives. `WorkRequestRecord` tests (replay, digest `conflict`, outcome updates, lost consume race as `confirmation_used`, confirmed resubmission), executor `started` and `still_unknown` tests, the late-settle `conflict` with audit and the hook `run_once()` tests (a sweeper failure fails startup) belong to slice 4 and slice 2. All four limits must be positive before work dispatches. No browser test (UI is E11-3 to E11-5).
