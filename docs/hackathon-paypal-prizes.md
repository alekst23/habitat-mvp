Amounts below are **per winning project**, not per team member. Multiple winners in one row each receive that row's award. A project can win more than one permitted award, so 23 awards does not mean 23 different winning projects.

## Which prizes can be combined

A project may receive **one grand prize OR one honorable mention**, together with **at most one sponsor prize**. It cannot combine a grand prize with an honorable mention or collect several sponsor awards. [Rules, section 8](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules)

The largest possible cash combination for one project is therefore **$17,000**, calculated as $12,000 overall first place plus $5,000 AG Grid first place. A $5,000 honorable mention plus AG Grid first place would total $10,000.

Our earlier `idea-01.md` prize strategy pairs overall first place and Most Impactful. They should be treated as alternative targets.

## Sponsor requirements and guidance

All sponsor awards require use of the corresponding sponsor's tools. The detail pages provide more specific direction than the broad eligibility statements in the prize table.

| Sponsor | What the sponsor page asks builders to demonstrate |
|---|---|
| [AG Grid](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/aggrid) | Emphasizes an AG Studio dashboard with custom widgets, theming, layouts and agent integration; discourages stopping at standalone grids or charts. Rules broadly allow AG Grid tools, but this guidance matters when choosing an implementation. |
| [APIMatic](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/apimatic) | Promotes the PayPal Context Plugin. The subscription claim form requires a repository or project showing plugin use and a description. Rules broadly allow APIMatic tools; the subscription claim is specifically tied to the plugin. |
| [Bryntum](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/bryntum) | Encourages effective use of Gantt, Scheduler and/or Calendar, such as timelines, bookings and agent-directed scheduling. |
| [Channel3](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/channel3) | Requires a meaningful feature calling its API or MCP server, such as product search, enrichment or recommendations. Mentioning Channel3 without an integration does not qualify. |
| [Render](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/details/render) | Directs builders to deploy on Render and use Render Workflows for orchestration. The rules phrase prize eligibility more broadly as using Render tools. |

APIMatic grants require manual approval, use the claimant's verified email and are nontransferable. See its [claim form and terms](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScc2oCgAFACm7d6H3R4mgzv-O34DdyVnLIBNiwmUNggr_-hhA/viewform). No separate numerical sponsor scoring rubric was found on the sponsor detail pages.

## Judging and award timing

The general judging criteria carry equal weight: technical implementation, design, potential impact, innovation and presentation. Sponsor eligibility is additional to the overall requirement to meaningfully combine PayPal and AI.

- Submission deadline: **November 12, 2026, noon Pacific / 3 p.m. Eastern**.
- Winner announcement: **on or around December 21, 2026**.
- Team prizes are paid to the designated representative for distribution.
- Required award forms must be returned within 10 business days; delivery is scheduled within 60 days after completed forms are received.

Source: [official rules, sections 1, 6 and 8](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules). This inventory records the current published schedule; refer to the live rules for subsequent changes.
